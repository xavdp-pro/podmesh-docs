# Lineage — PodMesh Documentation & Didactic Showcase (`podmesh-docs` v2.4.1)

## Universal Multi-Storage & Didactic Documentation (2026-10-28)

On **2026-10-28**, at release **v2.4.1**, `podmesh-docs` documents the transition from the experimental v2.4.1 demonstrator into the universal multi-platform era (Proxmox VE + TrueNAS + Synology + Windows + Podman), featuring FastCDC / BLAKE3 atomic chunk ingestion and WireGuard Dual-Hub anti-SPOF routing.

## Canonical Separation under SHAPER PHOENIX (2026-10-27)

On **2026-10-27**, at version **v2.3.0**, `podmesh-docs` was canonically separated under the **SHAPER PHOENIX** architecture from the unified `podmesh-vitrine` / `podmesh-manager` codebase:

- **`xavdp-pro/podmesh-docs` (`DIFFUSION/PUBLIC/podmesh-docs` — Public)**:
  - Serves `TechnicalShowcasePage` as the default root `/` route and `/cathedrale` (`FractalsPage`) as the 2D/3D Fractal Cathedral didactic simulator.
  - Dedicated to public technical documentation, architectural SVG diagrams, SLA validation explanations, and interactive sandbox exploration at `https://podmesh.xavdp.pro`.
- **`xavdp-pro/podmesh-cockpit` (`DIFFUSION/PRIVATE/podmesh-cockpit` — Private)**:
  - Serves `CockpitPage` as the primary root `/` route for restricted operator cluster administration.
